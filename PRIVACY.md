# Privacy Policy for AI Video Factory

Last updated: September 11, 2026

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy describes how **AI Video Factory** ("the App") handles information when you use our video automation service and connect with the TikTok Developer API.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **TikTok Account Information**: When you authorize the App using TikTok Login Kit, we receive basic public profile information (such as your display name and open ID) solely to identify your account and grant posting permissions.
- **Publishing Metadata**: Information necessary to publish videos, such as video title, description, privacy settings, and video media files.
- **Log Data**: Technical error logs and execution status stored locally to monitor video rendering and upload operations.

## 3. How We Use Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Authenticate and authorize your video publishing requests via TikTok's Content Posting API.
- Render, format, and upload approved video content to your TikTok account.
- Display status and analytics in your local dashboard.

## 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal data to third parties.
- Data sent to TikTok is handled directly in accordance with [TikTok's Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/privacy-policy/en).

## 5. Data Storage & Security
- All authentication tokens are stored securely in your private environment.
- No personal user data is permanently retained on external intermediate servers.

## 6. Your Rights & Revoking Access
You can revoke the App's permissions at any time via:
`TikTok App > Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Manage app permissions`.
